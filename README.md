## About the Movie
**Fabricated City** (Korean title: *Jo-jak-doen Do-si*) is a South Korean **action crime thriller** released in 2017. It follows an ordinary young man who is framed for a crime he didn't commit and has to fight to clear his name.

> "Sometimes the truth is the hardest thing to prove."
